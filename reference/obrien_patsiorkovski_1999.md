# O'Brien and Patsiorkovski (1999) rural Russia survey

Exercise dataset for Chapter 4, Exercise 4.6. Rural Russian households
surveyed over three years, used for an exercise in mock random
assignment.

## Usage

``` r
obrien_patsiorkovski_1999
```

## Format

A data frame with 462 rows and 8 columns:

- sexresp6:

  Respondent sex, coded 1 or 2

- ageresp6:

  Respondent age in years, 19 to 92

- group6:

  Group, 1 to 4

- memberc6:

  Membership indicator, coded 1 or 2

- index95:

  Outcome index, 1995 wave, 3 to 9

- index96:

  Outcome index, 1996 wave, 3 to 9

- index97:

  Outcome index, 1997 wave, 3 to 9

- randsort:

  Uniform random number, one per household, for sorting into a mock
  random assignment
