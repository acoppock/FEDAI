# Package index

## Population moments

Divide by N rather than N - 1. A schedule of potential outcomes is a
population, not a sample from one.

- [`pop_var()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/pop_var.md)
  : Population variance
- [`pop_cov()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/pop_cov.md)
  : Population covariance
- [`pop_sd()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/pop_sd.md) :
  Population standard deviation

## Standard errors and tests

The true standard error, which needs the potential outcomes no
experiment reveals, and the indirect test for effect heterogeneity.

- [`equation_3.4()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/equation_3.4.md)
  : The true standard error of a two-arm experiment under complete
  random assignment
- [`difference_in_variances()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/difference_in_variances.md)
  : Test for a difference in outcome variances between treatment arms

## Simulations

Bayesian updating from an experimental result, and a simulated research
literature in which studies arrive in a random order.

- [`posterior_mean_fn()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/posterior_mean_fn.md)
  : Compute the Bayesian posterior mean given a normal prior and
  experimental result
- [`simulate_posteriors()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/simulate_posteriors.md)
  : Simulate Bayesian posterior distributions across experimental sample
  sizes
- [`make_studies()`](https://alexandercoppock.com/FEDAI/reference/make_studies.md)
  : Simulate a research literature with a gender-composition moderator
