# Table 4.1 from FEDAI

Schedule of potential outcomes and pretest scores for a simulated
teacher incentives experiment.

## Usage

``` r
table_4_1
```

## Format

A data frame with 40 rows and 6 columns:

- observation:

  Subject number, 1 to 40

- X_pretest:

  Pretest score, strongly predictive of the outcome

- X_pretest_weak:

  Pretest score, weakly predictive of the outcome

- Z:

  Realized treatment assignment

- Y_Z_0:

  Untreated potential outcome

- Y_Z_1:

  Treated potential outcome
